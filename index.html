Privacy Policy for BlokNote

Effective Date: June 30, 2026

BlokNote ("the App") is developed to help users create, organize, and manage notes in a simple and secure way. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

BlokNote is designed to minimize data collection.

Information You Provide

  • Notes and content that you create within the app.
  • Optional account information, if you choose to create an account.

Information Collected Automatically

Depending on your device and app configuration, we may collect limited technical information such as:

  • Device type
  • Operating system version
  • App version
  • Crash reports
  • Anonymous usage statistics

This information is used solely to improve app stability and performance.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the app
  • Save and synchronize your notes (if cloud sync is enabled)
  • Improve performance and reliability
  • Fix bugs and technical issues
  • Respond to customer support requests

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited technical data with trusted third-party service providers that help operate the app, such as cloud storage, analytics, or crash reporting services. These providers may only use the information as necessary to perform services on our behalf.

4. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect your information. However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Children's Privacy

BlokNote is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that personal information has been collected from a child under 13, we will promptly delete it.

6. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as necessary to provide the app's functionality and for the purposes described in this policy:

  • Notes and content you create are stored on your device, and, if cloud sync or an account is enabled, on our backend for as long as your account or app installation remains active.
  • You can permanently delete your notes and account data at any time from within the app (Settings > Delete Account and Data). This action is immediate and removes your content from our systems.
  • If you uninstall the app without using the in-app delete option, locally stored data is removed from your device, and any associated account data is deleted automatically no later than 12 months after your last use of the app.
  • Technical information such as crash reports and anonymous usage statistics is retained for up to 12 months for stability and performance analysis, after which it is deleted or fully anonymized.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access your information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent where applicable

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the email below.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated Effective Date.
